## Cron & Heartbeat Monitoring

- [Cron-job.org](https://cron-job.org/) - Free cron job and HTTP endpoint monitoring service with scheduling and logging. Sends notifications via email, Telegram, or webhook when jobs fail. Offers a free tier and paid plans for more frequent execution. ([Read more](/details/cron-job-org.md)) `Cron` `Heartbeat` `HTTP Monitoring` `Free Plan` `Freemium`
- [Cronitor](https://cronitor.io/) - Monitors cron jobs, background tasks, and heartbeats by tracking ping-based signals. Alerts when jobs fail to start, run too long, or stop sending heartbeats. Freemium with paid plans for teams and advanced alerting. ([Read more](/details/cronitor.md)) `Cron` `Heartbeat` `Background Jobs` `Alerts` `Freemium`
- [Healthchecks.io](https://healthchecks.io/) - Cron and heartbeat monitoring service that listens for pings from scheduled tasks. Sends alerts via email, SMS, Slack, and other integrations when a ping is missed. Offers both hosted and self-hosted open-source options. ([Read more](/details/healthchecks-io.md)) `Cron` `Heartbeat` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `Freemium`
